Machine Learning Research Scientist — Scientific ML

We're hiring an ML research scientist to apply modern deep learning to physical-simulation problems. You'll design surrogate models, run training experiments, and publish results.

Responsibilities:
- Build PyTorch-based surrogate models for expensive physics simulations.
- Run gradient-based design optimization loops combining simulation and machine learning.
- Design and execute training experiments with proper baselines and ablations.
- Co-author papers and conference submissions.
- Collaborate with engineers to ship the resulting models into production pipelines.

Requirements:
- Strong PyTorch and Python skills.
- Experience training surrogate models, neural operators, or physics-informed neural networks.
- Comfort with automatic differentiation, JAX or PyTorch autograd.
- Background in optimization (gradient-based methods, topology optimization, design of experiments).
- Track record of publishing or submitting to peer-reviewed venues.
- Familiarity with finite-element analysis, computational mechanics, or scientific simulation.
