Content Marketing Manager — Brand Storytelling

We are looking for a Content Marketing Manager to lead brand storytelling across our owned, earned, and paid channels. You will define the editorial voice, build a content engine that compounds over time, and make sure every piece we publish ladders up to a coherent brand narrative. This role sits at the intersection of journalism, marketing, and design — equally comfortable shaping a customer story, briefing a designer, and reading a content-performance dashboard.

You will own the editorial calendar end-to-end and partner deeply with product marketing, demand gen, design, customer marketing, and executive leadership to plan, produce, and distribute content that drives awareness, affinity, and qualified pipeline.

Key job responsibilities

    Own the editorial strategy and calendar across blog, long-form reports, customer stories, video, podcast, newsletter, and social, with a clear point of view on what we publish, why, and for whom
    Develop and enforce a brand voice and style guide that every internal author and freelance contributor can apply consistently
    Lead production of flagship narrative content — annual reports, customer story films, executive bylines, and category-defining essays — from outline to publication
    Build a customer storytelling program that consistently turns adoption wins into reusable case studies, quotes, and reference assets
    Partner with SEO, demand gen, and lifecycle teams to ensure content is distributed, indexed, and repurposed across the funnel — not published-and-forgotten
    Brief, coach, and edit a mix of in-house contributors, freelance writers, and agency partners; raise the quality bar without becoming a single-threaded bottleneck
    Track content performance against business outcomes — organic traffic, engagement depth, pipeline contribution, brand lift — and use the data to retire what isn't working
    Represent the brand voice in cross-functional reviews, ensuring product launches, sales collateral, and executive communications stay on-tone

A day in the life

A typical day might include a customer interview for a long-form story, an edit pass on an executive byline, a planning session with design on the next quarterly report, and a debrief with demand gen on which content assets are pulling pipeline. You will be the person leadership trusts to turn a half-formed idea into a publishable piece that sounds like us.

Basic Qualifications

    6+ years of content marketing, editorial, or brand-journalism experience, including at least 2 years in a B2B technology setting
    Portfolio that demonstrates range — long-form narrative, customer storytelling, executive ghostwriting, and short-form social — with measurable outcomes attached
    Editor-quality writing and line-editing skills; able to elevate other people's drafts without flattening their voice
    Track record building a content function that scales beyond your own bandwidth — editorial calendars, briefs, style guides, freelance networks
    Comfort with content performance analytics and the discipline to kill underperforming formats

Preferred Qualifications

    Prior experience in journalism, magazine editorial, or in-house newsroom-style content teams
    Experience producing multi-format content programs (written, video, audio) and managing the production logistics that come with them
    Familiarity with SEO fundamentals, CMS platforms (Contentful, Sanity, or similar), and modern analytics stacks
    Background marketing to developers, designers, or other technical/creative audiences
    Experience working directly with founders or C-suite executives on thought-leadership content
