Product Marketing Manager — Developer Platform

We're hiring a Product Marketing Manager to own go-to-market strategy and positioning for our developer platform. You will partner with product, engineering, sales, and content teams to translate technical capabilities into compelling narratives that drive awareness, adoption, and revenue across both self-serve and enterprise motions.

This is a high-visibility role for someone who thrives at the intersection of technical depth and storytelling. You will define how we talk about the product, who we talk to, and where we show up — across launches, campaigns, sales enablement, analyst briefings, and customer-facing content.

Key job responsibilities

    Own end-to-end go-to-market planning for new product launches, including positioning, messaging, audience segmentation, launch tiers, and cross-functional readiness
    Develop differentiated positioning and messaging frameworks grounded in customer research, competitive analysis, and product capability deep-dives
    Partner with product management to influence roadmap based on market signal, win/loss analysis, and customer interviews
    Build and deliver sales enablement assets — pitch decks, battlecards, demo scripts, objection-handling guides, and ROI calculators — that materially improve seller productivity
    Collaborate with content, demand generation, and growth teams to translate positioning into campaigns, landing pages, blog posts, webinars, and customer stories
    Conduct primary and secondary research, including customer interviews, win/loss programs, and competitive teardowns, to surface insight that sharpens the narrative
    Brief and partner with industry analysts to shape category perception and earn favorable coverage in research notes
    Define and report on PMM KPIs: launch adoption, pipeline influence, sales attach rates, and content performance

A day in the life

In this role, you will move between strategic and tactical work. You might spend the morning interviewing a customer about their adoption journey, the afternoon iterating on a launch narrative with the product lead, and the early evening reviewing pitch deck revisions with sales leadership. You will be the connective tissue between what we build and how the market understands it.

Basic Qualifications

    5+ years of product marketing experience, with at least 2 years marketing a developer-facing or technical B2B product
    Demonstrated track record owning end-to-end product launches with measurable business impact
    Strong written communication — you can turn a dense technical spec into a one-pager that a CFO understands and a developer respects
    Experience building and delivering sales enablement content for technical buying committees
    Comfort working with quantitative data: funnel metrics, segmentation, A/B test results, and revenue attribution

Preferred Qualifications

    Background in software engineering, developer tools, infrastructure, AI/ML, or data platforms
    Experience marketing to developers, data scientists, or technical operators specifically
    Familiarity with PLG motions and self-serve onboarding funnels
    Experience briefing top-tier industry analysts (Gartner, Forrester, IDC)
    Portfolio demonstrating launch narratives, positioning frameworks, and analyst-grade collateral
    MBA or equivalent strategic-marketing training is a plus, but not required
